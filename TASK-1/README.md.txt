Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Retail Sales Data

About the Project

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of retail sales data. The goal is to understand sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and business insights.

Internship Details

- Organization: Oasis Infobyte
- Track: Data Analytics
- Level: 1
- Task: EDA on Retail Sales Data
- Intern Name: Anubhav Dubey

Tools and Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

Dataset

The dataset contains retail sales transactions from 2022 to 2025.

Analysis Performed

- Data loading and inspection
- Data cleaning and handling missing values
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales analysis
- Customer age and gender analysis
- Product and category performance analysis
- Payment method and order status analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Profit and sales visualizations

Key Findings

- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- Most customers belong to the 31–45 age group.
- AeroBook 14 Laptop had the highest sales.
- ProBook 16 Laptop generated the highest profit.
- Credit Card was the leading payment method.

Conclusion

The analysis helped identify important sales trends, customer patterns, and product performance.

Recommendations

1. Focus more on the Electronics category to increase revenue.
2. Create targeted offers for customers in the 31–45 age group.
3. Promote products with lower sales to improve their performance.
